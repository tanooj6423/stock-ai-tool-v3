# Equitex — Content & Acquisition Starter Kit
*Your "Varsity strategy": build trust with free content, convert the engaged. Nothing here is investment advice — it's education and transparency, which is exactly what keeps you registration-free.*

## The one rule that governs every post

Market the **tool, the method, and the track record** — never the outcome of an individual trade. Safe: "our screener flagged X at Equitex 71; here's the full methodology and how it played out." Unsafe (SEBI risk): "BUY X, target ₹Y 🎯". If a post could be read as a tip, rewrite it as education.

> 🧾 **Every setup from last week — wins AND losses**
> {STOCK1}: +{x}% ✅  ·  {STOCK2}: −{y}% ❌  ·  {STOCK3}: +{z}% ✅ ...

The losing weeks get posted too. That honesty is what makes it shareable and trustworthy — it's the opposite of every tipster channel, and it's why people will believe you.
